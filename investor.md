1. THE OPPORTUNITY & MARKET PROBLEM

Traditional ammonium nitrate fertilizers are vital for global agriculture but carry extreme logistical, environmental, and security risks. They are highly explosive, chemically unstable, and prone to rapid leaching, which severely pollutes groundwater. Governments worldwide are tightening regulations, creating an urgent market demand for a non-hazardous, eco-friendly, and high-efficiency substitute that does not compromise crop yields.

2. THE SOLUTION: NITROZINE PK™

NITROZINE PK is a revolutionary, eco-friendly granular NPK fertilizer engineered with a proprietary polymeric coating. It transforms volatile ammonium nitrate into a completely stable, non-explosive, and non-flammable compound, backed by national and international patents.
• Smart Slow-Release: Programmed to release essential nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium steadily over a 9-week cycle, maximizing root absorption and reducing environmental waste.
• Automated Custom Formulations: Fabricated through a specialized, automated manufacturing process that custom-tailors NPK ratios to the precise requirements of specific crops and soil compositions.
